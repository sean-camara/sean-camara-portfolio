# Media provenance

Prepared on September 5, 2026 for Sean John Camara’s portfolio.

- `aghimuan-live*.webp`: actual browser capture of https://www.aghimuancreatives.site/ at 1440 × 1000.
- `yhans-live*.webp`: actual browser capture of https://yhans-catering-services-eight.vercel.app/ at 1440 × 1000.
- 640 px and 960 px versions resize those captures without changing their interface content.
- Other WebP files are format/size optimizations of existing project screenshots, school photographs, and MongoDB credentials under `public/assets` and `public/certificates`.
- `sean-monogram.png` is an 80 px resize of the original `public/favicon.png`, used for the header and favicon. The original is retained unchanged.
- Original source files remain intact. No generated product screens, stock photographs, invented portraits, or third-party reference designs were introduced.
- The portfolio social image at `public/og.png` is a 1200 × 630 screenshot of the actual local redesign. `public/og.webp` is its encoded counterpart.
- The active fonts are the Latin subsets of Fraunces (upright and italic) and Manrope from Google Fonts. Their respective SIL Open Font License texts accompany the font files. The earlier Archivo file remains unused.

Capture source PNGs and verification evidence are in the gitignored `output/playwright/redesign/` directory.
